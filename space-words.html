How to play Space Words

Words written on space rocks fall towards your rocket. Start typing a word and the rock lights up purple; finish the word to blast it with your laser. If a rock reaches the bottom, you lose a ❤️. Blast all the rocks to win. Keep all 3 hearts for 3 ⭐ stars.

  • 3-letter words such as cat, sun and dog suit beginning readers (ages 6–7).
  • Sight words are the most common words in children's books (the, said, look, come). Typing them helps with reading as well. Children can read each word aloud as they type it.
  • Big words such as rainbow and dinosaur are a challenge for confident typists aged 8–10.

Questions parents ask

What age is Space Words for?

Space Words suits children aged 6 to 10 who know their letters and are starting to read. Beginners can pick 3-letter words on 🐢 Slow, while strong readers can blast big words like rainbow and dinosaur on 🚀 Fast.

Does typing words help with reading and spelling?

Yes. To type a word, your child has to look at every letter in order, which supports spelling. The sight words level uses the words that appear most often in early reading books, so practising them helps with reading as well.

What happens if my child makes a mistake?

Nothing bad. A wrong key gives a soft sound and the rock keeps falling, so your child simply presses the right key. If they started the wrong rock, Backspace lets them switch. Rocks only cost a ❤️ if they reach the rocket.
