About Tippy Typing

Tippy Typing is a free website that helps children aged 4 to 10 learn the keyboard through short lessons and playful games. It's made by Thornbyte, a small software studio in India.

What we believe

  • Free for every child. No paywalls on lessons or games.
  • Safe by design. No accounts, no chat, no personal data, and no ads inside games.
  • Little and often. Lessons take about 2 minutes, so young children finish feeling proud.
  • Right fingers first. Our stars reward accuracy, because speed follows good habits.
