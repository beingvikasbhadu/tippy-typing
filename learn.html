Typing Lessons

Each lesson takes about 2 minutes. Earn up to 3 ⭐ by typing carefully. Being correct matters more than being fast!

How the lessons work

Every lesson adds two new keys and mixes in the keys your child already knows. The coloured keyboard and the hands picture show which finger to use (there is also a printable finger chart). If your child presses a wrong key, the 🗣️ voice says the right one once as a gentle hint.

Mistakes don't stop the lesson. The letter turns red, and your child just presses the right key to carry on. Stars are based on how many keys were right the first time: 95% or more earns 3 stars, 85% earns 2, and finishing earns 1.
